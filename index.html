Chess, you are White

Tap one of your pieces, then a highlighted square. The bot plays Black, tap Play live to play a real friend in a Live room (with a clock if you want one), or pass and play on this device. Normal check rules: a king is never captured. If you are in check you must get out of it, and if a king is in check with no legal move left, that is checkmate. Castling and en passant are not in yet.

How to play chess here

Each piece moves the usual way: pawns forward (two squares on their first move), knights jump in an L, bishops diagonally, rooks straight, queens both ways, kings one square. Tap a piece to see its highlighted squares, then tap a highlighted square to move. A king is never taken off the board. If your king is in check, you must move it, capture the attacker, or block the attack. If a king is in check and there is no legal move left, that is checkmate and the game ends with an alert. The board squares are fixed now, so the tiles stay put while you play.

Whot, match shape or number

Play a card matching the top card by shape or number. Special cards: 1 = Hold On (play again), 2 = Pick Two (next player picks 2 or plays another 2), 8 = Suspension (next player misses a turn), 14 = General Market (the other player picks 1), 20 = Whot, wild, call a shape. First to empty their hand wins.

Whot rules in 30 seconds

Match the top card by shape or number, or play a wild Whot (20) and call the next shape. A 2 can only be answered with another 2, or you pick up the pending cards. A 1 lets you play again, an 8 makes the other player miss a turn, and a 14 makes the other player pick 1 card. Draw a card if you cannot play, then it is the other player’s turn.

Checkers, you are Blue

Tap a blue piece, then a highlighted square. Jump over a orange piece to capture it. Reach the far side to become a king and move both ways. Capture is compulsory, and a piece that can jump again must keep jumping. Tap Play live to play a real friend in a Live room, or pass and play on this device.

How to play checkers here

Your blue pieces move diagonally forward, one square at a time, only on the brown squares. Jump over a orange piece into the empty square behind it to capture it. If a capture is available you must take it, and if the same piece can jump again it must keep jumping in the same turn. Reach the far side and your piece becomes a king (marked K) and can move backwards too. The board squares are fixed now, so the tiles stay put while you play.

How to play Ludo-style Race

Tap Roll for you and watch the dice tumble before it settles. Move that many squares. Then the bot rolls its own dice, in its own turn, and you can watch that roll too. Land exactly on the bot and it goes back 5 squares, and it can do the same to you. First to square 40 wins.

Dominoes, match either end

Play a tile that matches the left or right end of the chain. Draw from the boneyard if you’re stuck. Lowest hand total when the round ends wins.

New to Dominoes? The 30-second version

A domino tile has two numbers, like [3 | 5]. The chain on the table has two ends. You may play a tile only if one of its numbers matches an end. Example: if the chain ends are 3 and 5, you can play [3 | 6] on the 3 end, or [5 | 2] on the 5 end. Only tiles that match an end are tappable here, the rest are dimmed, so you cannot play a wrong tile by accident. If nothing matches, tap Draw tile. Empty your hand first to win the round; if nobody can play, the lowest hand total wins.
